How to use these in your deck

  • Present live: open a file full-screen (F11) and screen-share it as a slide.
  • Put it in Keynote / PowerPoint / Canva: screen-record the tab (e.g. macOS Cmd+Shift+5) and drop the clip/GIF onto a slide.
  • Embed live: <iframe src="orbit.html"> in a web deck.
  • Clean capture: press H to hide on-screen labels/hints; on the orbit, press T for a transparent background.
  • Edit the copy: all text is plain HTML — replace [ COMPANY ], the specs, and the metrics with your real numbers.

⚠ Specs, altitudes & metrics are placeholders — swap in your real figures before pitching.
